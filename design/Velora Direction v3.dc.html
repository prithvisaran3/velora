Our Story — rewritten, in your mother's voice
1977 — TODAY · ERODE
My father taught me silk before he taught me arithmetic.
என் தந்தை எனக்கு கணக்கு சொல்லும் முன்பே பட்டு சொல்லிக் கொடுத்தார்.

In 1977 my father, Mahadevan, opened a thousand square feet of silk on a busy road in Erode with his brother beside him and very little else. He was not a designer and he never called himself one. He was a man who could tell you, with his eyes shut and the cloth between two fingers, which loom a saree came off and whether the zari would hold its colour after ten years of being folded in a steel trunk.

I grew up behind that counter. My school holidays were spent sorting bundles by weight, learning why a four-inch border needs a heavier body, why some reds go dull under tube light and some come alive. When I was nineteen he began sending me to the weavers alone. He never once asked me what I had bought. He would only unfold it, hold it to the door light, and nod — or not nod.

Thirty years later, I have chosen sarees for three generations of the same families — the grandmother's muhurtham, the daughter's reception, the granddaughter's first Friday at the temple. I know which of them will call me before a wedding and ask me to keep something aside. That trust was not built by advertising. It was built one saree at a time, by never selling a woman something I would not wear myself.

Velora is that same hand, now reaching further. Every saree here is one I have held to the light myself. There is only ever one of each. If I would not put it in my own trunk, it does not go on this website.

Priya Mahadevan

WHAT IS WRONG WITH THE LIVE SITE
1. My developer annotations shipped as customer-facing copy — "hero video · 8s silent loop", "background-color 800ms cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)", "prefers-reduced-motion → instant swap". Those must never render.
2. Prices are ₹2,450–₹4,800, so the single-price promise is gone. Decide: one price, or show a range honestly.
3. Every image is still a striped placeholder — no photography, no drape video, so nothing moves and nothing sells.
4. No loader, no page transitions, no dye, no 3D. The motion spec was never built.
5. "BY BHARANI PATTU", "LAKSHMI, SECOND GENERATION" and "since 1978" all need removing.
