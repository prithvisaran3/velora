V2 · SAME PALETTE · NEW NAME PLATE · SIX 3D MOMENTS
Six places where 3D earns its weight
Saffron and marigold stay exactly as approved. What changes: the endorsement is now by Priya Mahadevan, the V is a full capital, Our Story is rewritten in Priya's voice around her father Mahadevan — and every screen below gains a real 3D moment. Each one is specified with its placement, its technique, its fallback and its frame budget, because 3D that costs the first product image is 3D we cut.
01 · extruded vel, 0.6 rotation → flat, then wipe
The vel loader, in three dimensions
The mark is a real extruded solid on a dark stage. It turns 0.6 of a revolution, catches one specular sweep along the spine, settles perfectly flat into the 2D lockup — then the saffron fabric wipe pulls up and the page is there.
PLACEMENT first visit only, sessionStorage
TECH R3F + extrudeGeometry from the SVG path, MeshPhysicalMaterial, one env light
FALLBACK 2D stroke-draw loader (already specified)
BUDGET 900ms hard cap · never blocks the hero image
02 · cloth solve, 24×24 grid, settles in 1.4s
Hero — a saree that actually falls
Not a video. A cloth-simulated plane with an anisotropic silk shader: sheen runs across the weft, zari threads take a sharper specular. It falls from just above frame on load, settles, then breathes with the pointer — move left, the pallu lifts. Headline sits over it, never on top of the fold.
PLACEMENT home hero, desktop and mobile
TECH R3F, custom sheen shader (Blinn anisotropic), 24×24 verlet cloth, DPR clamped 1.5
FALLBACK poster image + 8s video loop under 480p on low-end devices
BUDGET mounts AFTER LCP · scene ≤ 180 KB · 60fps target, 30fps floor
03 · same mesh, material re-tinted in 800ms
Shop by Colour — the cloth dyes, not the CSS
Choosing maroon lerps the fabric material's base colour and sheen tint over 800ms while the page ground follows. It reads as dye taking to silk rather than a div changing colour. Zari stays gold through every hue — that is what makes it look like cloth and not a filter.
PLACEMENT /colour/[slug], one shared canvas across navigations
TECH uniform lerp on the same mesh; no re-mount, no re-upload of textures
FALLBACK the approved CSS page dye
BUDGET zero additional download between colours
04 · orbitable drape + macro loupe camera
PDP — walk around the drape
The saree is draped on a simple 3D form: drag to orbit 40° either side, and the border stays legible at every angle. The loupe becomes a second camera rendering a 3× macro view of the weave under the cursor — real thread detail, not a scaled bitmap.
PLACEMENT product page, below the flat-lay hero — the hero stays a plain img
TECH one GLB form (≤ 400 KB) + per-saree 2K texture; second render target for the loupe
FALLBACK draped photo + the 2D loupe already specified
BUDGET lazy on intersection · desktop and high-end mobile only
05 · scroll unrolls the roll: body → border → pallu
The pallu unroll
A rolled saree in 3D. Scroll unrolls it across the viewport — the mesh literally unwinds off the cylinder — while marigold annotations arrive on the body, the four-inch border and the mangai pallu. This is the section that teaches a 28-year-old what she is buying.
PLACEMENT home, one section; also /story as the 2026 panel
TECH GSAP ScrollTrigger driving a single uniform (0→1) on a bent-plane shader
FALLBACK the approved 2D horizontal pallu strip; mobile gets staggered stills
BUDGET desktop only · dynamic import · pinned 120vh maximum
BAG
06 · fold on two axes, arc to the bag, gold pulse
Add to bag, and the zari stepper
The product image folds like cloth along two axes and arcs into the bag, which pulses once in marigold. On the tracking page the same material logic draws the zari thread: each newly completed segment is woven in, warp then weft, over 900ms.
PLACEMENT PDP and sticky mobile bar · /track/[reference]
TECH FLIP measurement + a 2-fold plane in R3F for the flight; SVG stroke weave for the stepper
FALLBACK 720ms CSS transform flight; stepper segment simply appears
BUDGET no new canvas — reuses the hero renderer when present
THE RULE THAT KEEPS THIS FAST
One WebGL context for the whole site, created after LCP, handed between routes. Every scene declares a poster twin. Device tiering: high (full scenes), mid (hero + dye only), low (posters and CSS). prefers-reduced-motion collapses to posters everywhere.
WHAT PRIYA MUST SUPPLY
One flat-lay per saree (2000px, even light), one 5s drape clip, and a 2K weave crop for the loupe and the 3D texture. Without the weave crop, moments 02–05 fall back to posters — the 3D is only as good as the cloth photography feeding it.

My father, Mahadevan, opened his first shop with his brother beside him and very little else. I was nineteen. He never asked what I had bought — he would unfold it, hold it to the door light, and nod, or not nod. Thirty years later I am still buying the way he taught me.

PERFORMANCE RULES
Hero poster is a real <img> with fetchpriority=high; video attaches after LCP. Drape loops are 3s, ≤400 KB, muted, playsinline, loaded only when the card is within one viewport. Loupe zoom uses a single 1600px crop, not a tile pyramid. Pallu scroll is desktop-only and lazy-mounted.
REDUCED MOTION
prefers-reduced-motion → loader shows the finished mark for 200ms, wipes become 120ms cross-fades, drape loops show poster frames with a play control, colour dye is instant, dividers appear without drawing. Nothing is lost, only stilled.
